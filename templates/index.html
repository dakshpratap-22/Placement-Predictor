What you can do

📊

Placement Prediction

A Logistic Regression model, trained by train_model.py, estimates whether a profile is likely to be Placed or Not Placed, with a probability.

🎯

Role Readiness

Compare your skills with a transparent checklist for Python Developer, Data Analyst or Web Developer and get learning tips and project ideas.

🔄

What-If Simulation

Change CGPA, projects, internships, backlogs or skills and see original vs hypothetical results side by side, recalculated by the real model.

🔒

Private accounts

Register and log in to keep your own prediction history. Other users can never see your records.

📈

Analytics reports

Charts of your trend, outcome distribution, current-vs-previous comparison, what-if results and role readiness — all from your saved records.

⇆

Compare history

Pick any two saved predictions and see exactly which inputs and model outputs changed.

Please note: this project uses a clearly labelled synthetic dataset, not real student placement records. Results are demonstrations of the technique, not evidence of real-world accuracy or a promise of employment.
